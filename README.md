# All-In-One Thoroughbred Raising

An all-in-one thoroughbred mod that introduces breeding, training, and racing of horses. Tailored to capture realism and adrenaline.

*   **Official Wiki:** [https://aiothoroughbred.fandom.com/wiki/All-In-One\_Thoroughbred\_Raising\_Wiki](https://aiothoroughbred.fandom.com/wiki/All-In-One_Thoroughbred_Raising_Wiki) (Not always up to date, check the guidebook for more reliable information)
*   **Languages:** English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.

\======================================================================

## Overview

All-In-One Thoroughbred Raising combines breeding, training, daily stable care, and competitive racing into one cohesive system. Breed champion horses with real pedigrees, train them to reach greater heights, and put your work to the test on the track.

Designed for realism, these are no longer vanilla horses you can push around without consequence. Every decision matters - one wrong call can mean another day for your prized horse, or the end of its career and life. Make your decisions wisely.

\======================================================================

## Core Features

*   **Overhauled Riding & Racing Physics:** Manage Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, and Wits alongside Distance Aptitude zones, Kick Reserve, running strategies (including Big Escape, Front Runner, Pace Chaser, Late Surger, End Closer, and Tactical), drafting, cornering, and rhythmic Racing Crop urging.
*   **Smart AI Jockeys & Race Management:** Build full racecourses with starting gates, tote boards, photo finishes, scheduled races, and 9-variant betting (`/tb schedule`). AI jockeys ride with full physics parity against players, avoiding kickback, drafting on turf, and reading race pace dynamically.
*   **Training & Conditioning:** Press `;` for Training Mode on track, or lead horses into water for zero-limb-stress pool training. Manage Body Weight, Mare Physique, Muscular Frame, Mood, and Fatigue.
*   **Breeding, Genetics & Personality:** Register horses under BHA (English), HKJC (Chinese), or JRA (Japanese) naming rules. Pass down pedigrees, traits, skills, Equine Personas, coat/eye genetics, and manage herd bonds, rivalries, and foal handling.
*   **Realistic Health, Injuries & Mortality:** Monitor Hunger, Hydration, Fatigue, 10 Ailments, and 5 Limb Injuries. Call `/tb vet` for treatment, use `/tb shop` for supplies, or rely on Enchanted Golden Apples as an expensive near-panacea. Catastrophic fractures (Poor Prognosis) and old age (25+ Equine Years) carry real life-or-death stakes.
*   **Endgame & Server Progression:** Send horses on Overseas Expeditions (`/tb overseas`), offer Stud Services (`/tb stud`), and immortalize champions with Hall of Fame Statues and trophies.
*   **In-Game Guidebook:** Run `/tb guide` at any time for the built-in 5-volume handbook covering controls, stats, care, racing, and commands.

## Quick Start

1.  **Choose a World Equine Time Flow:** On world creation, pick One-Year (real-life timescale), Season (3 or 4-month cycles), or Custom. Once chosen for a world, it cannot be changed.
2.  **Register as a Horse Owner:** Earn $10,000 using Lightman's Currency (1 Copper Coin = $1), deposit it with `/tb cashin`, and run `/tb owner` to register.
3.  **Register a Thoroughbred:** Craft a Thoroughbred Registry Form and use it on a tamed vanilla horse (or a DLO Thoroughbred if DragN's Livestock Overhaul! is installed). Sneak + right-click your horse anytime to view its Stat Card.
4.  **Train, Care & Race:** Press `;` to toggle Training Mode, buy feed and tack via `/tb shop`, and check `/tb guide` or the Wiki whenever you need details on stats, medical care, or race hosting.

## Requirements & Compatibility

*   **Minecraft / Loader:** Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge)
*   **Required Dependency:** Lightman's Currency (used for the economy, owner registration, vet fees, betting, and `/tb shop`. While not installing this mod falls back to using vanilla emeralds, it is not recommended.)
*   **Optional Soft-Dependency:** DragN's Livestock Overhaul! (DLO) 3.9.4 - adds full support for DLO Thoroughbreds with 3D tack (blinkers, shadow rolls, weighted pads, polo wraps, saddlecloths), 3D AI jockeys, Studbook 3D previews, and DLO feed/soup support. All other 23 DLO horse breeds remain completely untouched, and the mod still works 100% standalone with vanilla horses.
